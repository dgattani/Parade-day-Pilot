    What the agent did

      Data review: what to trust

      • Confirmed for 2026: Thu Nov 26, 8:30 AM to about noon, same 2.5-mile route: Central Park West at 77th St → Central Park South → 6th Ave → 34th St at Macy's. About 3.5M spectators expected.
      • Not yet confirmed: exact closure times. The app uses the city's 2025 times (most roads reopen about 3 PM; 7th Ave near Herald Sq until 8 PM). NYPD can change them on the day.
      • Gap in public data: no official list of where people on foot can cross the route. The app assumes no crossing while the parade is moving.
      • Map shapes are approximate, drawn by hand from street corners.
      • Google Routes has no "avoid this area" option. The agent asks for several routes, checks each against the closure zones, and adds a detour stop if needed. Subway trips only have their walking parts checked.

      Sources: Fox5 NY 2026 closures map, Afar 2026 parade guide, NYC 2025 street-closure notice, MTA Thanksgiving 2025 service notice, Google Maps Platform docs.
